Home Health · AI Playbook

Never miss another after hours call.

Most home health agencies lose referrals and worried families to voicemail after 5 p.m. Here's how to close that gap with AI, in an afternoon.

3 min readBy Simerson Ai

Here's the quiet leak in almost every home health agency: the phone rings at 7 p.m. It's a discharge planner with a referral, or a daughter who's scared about her dad. Nobody picks up. They leave a voicemail, or they don't, and they call the next agency on the list.

You can't staff a front desk around the clock. But you also can't afford to keep missing the calls that turn into admissions. This is the exact gap AI is good at closing right now, and you can set up a basic version this week.

The 3 step setup

1
Decide what a good call captures. Write down the five things you need from every after hours call: who's calling, the patient, what they need, how urgent it is, and the best callback number. That short list is the "script" your AI will follow.
2
Put an AI voice assistant on your after hours line. Tools like this answer in a warm, natural voice, ask your five questions, and never put anyone on hold. Forward your main line to it after business hours, no new number to hand out.
3
Split urgent from everything else. Anything that sounds time sensitive gets texted to your on call person immediately. The rest lands in your inbox as a tidy summary, waiting for you at 8 a.m., not lost in a voicemail box.

A missed call isn't a missed call. It's a referral that went to the agency that answered.

Keep it compliant

Two rules make this safe in healthcare: never let the AI give medical advice or make a clinical decision, and keep a real person in the loop for anything urgent. If the tool will touch patient information, make sure the vendor signs a BAA. That's the line between a helpful assistant and a liability.

That's it. A warm voice that always answers, the right calls escalated, and a clean list every morning. Most owners who do this stop losing after hours referrals within the first week.
