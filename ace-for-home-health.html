The daily grind

Referrals shouldn't pile up on a desk.

In most agencies, every referral is read by hand — eligibility checked, docs chased, auth verified — one PDF at a time, while the clock runs and the next agency calls the patient first.

Hand-screening eats clinical hours

A nurse reading every referral for eligibility and coverage is a nurse not seeing patients — the most expensive data-entry you can run.

A missed detail is a denied claim

A face-to-face gap or the wrong authorization, caught weeks later, means clawed-back revenue — and a compliance problem on the record.
