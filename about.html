About

Software should do the busywork. People make the calls.

SimersonAi builds custom AI agents that take the repetitive, time-draining work off your team's plate, so the people you hired can spend their hours where they matter.

Why we exist

The work caught up to the technology.

Every business runs on a layer of invisible, repetitive work, the follow ups, the data entry, the scheduling, the status updates. It rarely gets automated because it's spread across tools, full of judgment calls, and never quite urgent enough to fix.

SimersonAi exists to close that gap. We design AI agents around the way your business actually works, a clear trigger, the right inputs, supervised steps, and a human approval point before anything that matters goes out the door.

The result isn't a tool your team has to learn. It's work that simply happens, reliably, on the record, and under your control.

The founder
“I spent years watching good teams stay late doing work a computer should have handled. I started SimersonAi to build exactly what I wished we'd had, and to build it for the people living inside that work.”

SimersonAi was founded on a simple conviction: the most valuable automation isn't the flashiest. It's the quiet kind that gives people their time back. Every engagement starts the same way, by sitting with how your business really runs before a single line of an agent is built.
