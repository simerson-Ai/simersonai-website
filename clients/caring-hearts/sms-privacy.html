Caring Hearts Premier Home Care

SMS Messaging Privacy Policy

Effective June 16, 2026

What this covers

This policy explains how Caring Hearts Premier Home Care ("Caring Hearts", "we", "us") collects and uses information in connection with our text message (SMS) program for caregivers, operated on our behalf by our technology provider, SimersonAi (Simerson Industries LLC).

The text messages we send

Enrolled caregivers receive: (1) one-time verification codes, and (2) notices that a shift is available, with a secure link to view details and accept. We do not send marketing or promotional texts. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

How caregivers opt in

Caregivers provide their mobile number and give written consent during onboarding with Caring Hearts (a signed caregiver agreement / onboarding form with an SMS-consent checkbox). Consent is collected before any message is sent and is not a condition of purchase. Caregivers may opt out at any time by replying STOP, or get help by replying HELP. See the Caregiver SMS Consent page.

Information we collect for the SMS program

Mobile phone number, opt-in/opt-out status and timestamps, and message delivery logs. We do not collect message content beyond what is needed to operate and audit the dispatch service.

How we use it

Only to operate the caregiver-dispatch text program described above (send shift offers and verification codes, honor opt-outs, and keep a compliance audit trail).

Sharing — and our SMS commitment

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information may be shared with subcontractors that provide support services (such as our messaging platform SimersonAi and our carrier Telnyx) solely to deliver these messages. Text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties for any other purpose. We do not sell mobile information.

Data protection

This SMS program is operated under HIPAA-aligned controls. No protected health information (PHI) is ever placed in a text message — texts contain only a shift area/time and a secure portal link; client names, conditions, and addresses never appear in an SMS.

Your choices

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe (you'll get a confirmation and no further texts). Reply START to resubscribe. Reply HELP for help.
