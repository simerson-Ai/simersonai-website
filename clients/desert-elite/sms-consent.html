SMS Consent & Terms

Text messages about your ride and payment

When you call Desert Elite Medical Transportation ("Desert Elite", "we", "us") to book a ride, you can choose to receive text messages about your trip and payment. This page explains what those messages are and how your consent is recorded. The program is operated on our behalf by our technology provider, SimersonAi (Simerson Industries LLC).

How you consent

You give consent verbally, on the phone, when you call Desert Elite to arrange a ride and provide your mobile number. Our automated booking assistant discloses that it is an AI and tells you that trip confirmations and, for private-pay rides, a secure payment link will be texted to the number you provide. The call is recorded. No text is sent unless you have called in and agreed on the call. Consent is never a condition of purchase.

The messages you may receive

Ride booking confirmations and reminders, and — for private-pay rides — a secure link to pay for your trip. We do not send marketing or promotional texts. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Confirmation text

Example of a booking confirmation:

Desert Elite: Your ride is confirmed. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

Keyword replies

STOP → Desert Elite: you're unsubscribed and won't receive more messages. Reply START to resubscribe.

HELP → Desert Elite: For help call (760) 289-8288 or email Dispatch@desertelitetransportation.com. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out.

Sharing & our SMS commitment

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information may be shared with subcontractors that provide support services (such as our messaging platform SimersonAi and our carrier Telnyx) solely to deliver these messages. Text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties for any other purpose. We do not sell mobile information.

Your choices

Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe. Reply START to resubscribe. Reply HELP for help. See also our SMS Privacy Policy.
