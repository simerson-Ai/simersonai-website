Diamond Home Care — caregiver scheduling texts

Diamond Home Care (Indio, California) uses an automated scheduling assistant, operated by SimersonAi, LLC, to send its own caregivers (employees) text messages about their work shifts from (760) 907-0425.

Who gets these texts

Only Diamond Home Care caregivers and office managers who have given their mobile number to Diamond Home Care and agreed to receive scheduling texts. We never text the public, never buy or rent numbers, and never send marketing or promotional messages.

How caregivers opt in

  • On the caregiver information form. When a caregiver joins Diamond Home Care, they enter their mobile number and check the consent box shown below. Checking the box is optional and is not a condition of employment.
  • By text. A caregiver can text START to (760) 907-0425 at any time to opt in.

Consent statement (exactly as shown to caregivers):

“By checking this box, I agree to receive text messages from Diamond Home Care at the mobile number I provided about my work schedule, including shift reminders, shift changes, open-shift requests and clock-in confirmations. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of employment. Messaging is operated by SimersonAi, LLC. See the Privacy Policy and Terms at simersonai.com.”

Opt-in form (caregiver information form — text section)

Yes — text me about my work schedule from Diamond Home Care. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of employment.

The box is never pre-checked. This copy of the form is for reference only.

What we send

  • A reminder about one hour before a scheduled shift
  • Shift changes and schedule confirmations
  • Requests to cover an open shift (reply YES or NO)
  • Confirmation that a clock-in or clock-out text was received

Messages never include patient or client health information. Frequency varies with each caregiver's schedule — typically a few messages a week.

Diamond Home Care: Hi Maria, reminder — your shift today starts at 8:00 AM. Reply STOP to opt out.
Diamond Home Care: Hi Ruth, can you pick up a shift Tue, Nov 10, 8a–12p? Reply YES or NO. Reply STOP to opt out.

Opting out and getting help

  • Reply STOP (or UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT) to stop all texts. You'll get one confirmation and nothing more.
  • Reply START to opt back in.
  • Reply HELP for help, or call (760) 907-0425, or email info@simersonai.com.

Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Numbers are used only to send the scheduling messages described above. See the Privacy Policy and Terms.
